Five things to do after reading Crabby and the Perfect Hiding Place. Scroll down and give them a try!

1. Crabby's Word Search

Click a letter, then click the last letter of a word to select the whole line. Find all 10 words hiding in the grid.

    Read each sentence about the story and pick True or False.

      4. Colour In Crabby
