Crabby goes on the Plane

Five things to do after reading Crabby goes on the Plane. Scroll down and give them a try!

1. Crabby's Word Search

Click a letter, then click the last letter of a word to select the whole line. Find all 10 words hiding in the grid.

    You found them all! Great eyes. 🦀

    2. True or False?

    Read each sentence about the story and pick True or False.

    3. Put It In Order

    Drag (using the ☰ handle) or tap the arrows to move these moments from Crabby goes on the Plane into the order they happened in the story.

      That's the right order — well done! 🦀

      4. Colour In Crabby

      Print this page (or just this picture) and colour in Crabby waving goodbye as his plane flies off to London — he's not always red!


      5. Your Turn!

      Crabby had a very busy flight, and nobody saw a thing! Where would you like Crabby to fly to next, and what might he get up to on the way? Write (or dictate to a grown-up) your own Crabby story idea below, then print it out to keep.
