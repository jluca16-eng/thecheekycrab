Welcome to the Adventures of Crabby

Crabby is a small red Lego crab who lives in a crevice on a brown rock of a Lego Beach Club set. He belongs to Sofia Kontorinis, a nine-year-old girl who loves adventure and has a wonderful imagination.

Crabby is a mischievous crab who also loves adventure. He interacts with Sofia's world in a very strange way. He stows away nestled deep in her ponytail, which allows him to experience her adventures firsthand without anyone ever noticing that he is there. Crabby also has adventures in the world of Lego, where he explores Sofia's many Lego sets and befriends other Lego characters.

I hope you enjoy reading about the wonderful adventures of Crabby.
